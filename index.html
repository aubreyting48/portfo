Gig X Match - Brand & Logo Identity

Brand visual identity and geometric logo design for an on-demand creative marketplace.

Project Overview

Created the core visual identity and logo system for Gig X Match, an on-demand marketplace tailored for the creative arts and entertainment industries. The platform instantly connects event organizers with creative talent for project-based gigs.

Design Criteria & Color Strategy

  • Keywords: Professional, Minimalist, Lively / Active.
  • Shape Language: Clean, modern geometric forms utilizing smooth rounded corners rather than overly fancy/ornate structures.
  • Strategic Color Consultation: The client initially requested Purple (Primary) and Indigo (Secondary). I recommended replacing Indigo with a vibrant Yellow to better convey the energetic, lively nature of the creative industry and elevate brand contrast.

Logo Concept Evolution & Ideation

  • Initial Draft ("X" Concept): Focused on the letter "X" from the brand name to visually symbolize intersecting pathways and matching.
  • Final Identity ("M" Concept): Evolved through iterative client reviews into a dynamic concept featuring intersecting spotlight beams casting light across multiple dimensions. The converging light beams dynamically form the letter "M", echoing the core identity of "Match" and the stage lighting of the entertainment world.

Logo Design Brand Identity Vector Illustration Color Theory & Strategy Creative Direction

Key Takeaways & Learnings

  • Translating Strategy to Design: Successfully guided a client to refine their original color vision, demonstrating how color psychology aligns brand identity with industry context.

AI-Assisted Website Audits & Revamp Prototypes

Human review plus AI assistance to turn live-site issues into audit reports and revamp prototypes.

Project Overview

I reviewed existing organisation websites, spotted current UX, accessibility, content, and technical issues, then used AI as an assistant to structure those findings into audit reports. Where a revamp was the next step, I asked AI to help generate design directions and HTML prototypes—which I then checked, edited, and presented as a clearer before-and-after path.

AI accelerated the documentation and first-pass mockups. I remained responsible for the brief, verifying issues on the live site, and deciding what to recommend.

My Role & Workflow

  • Human review first: Inspected live sites for layout, mobile use, navigation, accessibility, SEO/GEO signals, performance, and conversion friction.
  • AI-assisted audit reports: Combined my findings with AI to produce structured reports (health score, critical errors, UX recommendations, and suggested fixes).
  • AI-assisted prototypes: Used AI to generate homepage/key-page concepts and HTML prototypes so stakeholders could see what a revamp might look like before a rebuild.
  • Judgment stays human: I defined the audit brief, rejected weak suggestions, kept authentic photography where it mattered, and owned the final recommendation.

Case Studies

  • Review target 1: Educational-tour site on Wix. Key issues included a missing mobile menu, broken or misleading tour links, inconsistent buttons, and a hero that did not explain who they are. Delivered an audit report plus HTML homepage prototypes.

Technologies & Skills

Website Audit UX / Accessibility Review AI-Assisted Reporting AI-Assisted Prototyping HTML / CSS Prototypes Stakeholder Communication

Key Takeaways & Learnings

  • AI as an assistant, not the author: Reports and prototypes move faster with AI, but a designer still has to verify issues on the live site and decide what is actually worth fixing.
  • Show the problem and the direction: Pairing an audit score with a visible prototype helps non-technical stakeholders understand why a revamp is needed—and what “better” could look like.
