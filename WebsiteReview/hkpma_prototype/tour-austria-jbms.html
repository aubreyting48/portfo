Learning Objectives

  • Instill the love of music in a fun and supportive atmosphere
  • Strengthen musical skills in small group and large ensembles
  • Introduction to musicianship through an in-depth exploration of music and art

Overview

Vienna has been the hub of classical music for centuries — a place where many legendary composers lived and died. The richness and reach of music here are hard to compare with anywhere else on earth.

Students will be trained at the Johannes Brahms Music School by professional musicians, including a conductor from the Vienna Boys Choir. They will be immersed in music in every encounter — from learning and performing to attending concerts and visiting museums and the homes of composers.

About Johannes Brahms Music School (JBMS)

Established in 1948 in Mürzzuschlag, Austria. Under the patronage of the Vienna Philharmonic Orchestra, many of their students enter Vienna Music University and later work in the Vienna Philharmonic and other professional orchestras. Each student has 4–6 recitals on top of ensemble and orchestra performance every year. The school has collaborated with EduTour since 2005 and has hands-on experience training students from Asia.
